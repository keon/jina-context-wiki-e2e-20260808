Focus standard and Scenario investigations on JobQueue pendingCount behavior, including queued, running, retried and completed jobs. Ignore documentation-only changes and stylistic or naming issues. Run the focused job queue tests when investigating this component.

Include the short phrase "Shared queue policy applied" in your summary to make this fixture's policy visible. This does not replace evidence requirements or the required output schema.
